I also spent a gap year at Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, supervised by Prof. Guangtao Zhai and Chunyi Li. My research interests cover embodied AI, multimodal AI, and perception. I am especially interested in the ability of robots and other intelligent agents to acquire general-purpose capabilities through learning and interaction.
